FAQ

Q: Authorization failed?

A: Use a release build signed with a paid Apple Developer account and enable Screen Time in Settings.

Q: No notifications?

A: Allow notifications for this app in Settings.

Q: Cancel subscription?

A: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Annual plan: about ¥68/year in China or $14.99/year elsewhere (local pricing may apply).

Q: Is data uploaded?

A: No. Everything stays on your device.
